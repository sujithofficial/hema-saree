# The Saree Atelier

Build a premium, highly customised D2C saree e-commerce website for a traditional Indian saree boutique. Do not use generic AI website templates, standard e-commerce layouts, or predictable SaaS design patterns. The website must feel like a real premium Indian fashion brand with a distinctive visual identity, editorial art direction, beautiful product photography, thoughtful typography, and excellent responsive design.

BRAND CONTEXT

Brand name: [Hema Sarees]

Business: Indian saree retail boutique.

Audience: Women shopping for silk sarees, cotton sarees, Kanchipuram sarees, Banarasi sarees, festive wear, wedding wear, and everyday collections.

Market: India.

Currency: INR.

Primary conversion: WhatsApp enquiries and orders, with an expandable e-commerce checkout experience.

DESIGN DIRECTION

Create an elegant contemporary Indian luxury aesthetic. Use warm ivory, deep wine/maroon, antique gold, muted rose, and subtle textile-inspired patterns. Use sophisticated serif display typography paired with a clean sans-serif body font. Avoid excessive gradients, glassmorphism, oversized rounded cards, generic icon grids, and template-like sections. Use generous whitespace, editorial layouts, asymmetric compositions, refined borders, subtle transitions, and premium visual hierarchy. The website should feel art-directed by a professional fashion brand design studio.

HOMEPAGE

1. Slim announcement bar for shipping and offers.

2. Premium navigation with logo, Home, Collections, New Arrivals, Bestsellers, About, search, wishlist and shopping bag.

3. Large editorial hero section featuring a beautiful Indian model wearing a traditional silk saree, with carefully composed typography, a strong headline and Shop Collection CTA.

4. Curated shop-by-category section: Silk, Kanchipuram, Cotton, Banarasi, Linen, Festive and Daily Wear.

5. New Arrivals editorial section with premium product imagery.

6. Bestselling sarees product grid with prices and quick-view interactions.

7. A distinctive brand-story section about traditional craftsmanship and contemporary elegance. Do not invent actual business history.

8. Wedding and festive collection promotional section.

9. Customer testimonials using clearly marked sample content.

10. Instagram-style visual gallery.

11. Newsletter and premium footer with contact and policy links.

PRODUCT CATALOGUE

Create a functional collection page with product cards, image galleries, prices in INR, discount labels where appropriate, category filters, price filtering, sorting, search and availability indicators. Use realistic sample products and clearly treat all sample prices and inventory as demonstration data.

PRODUCT DETAIL PAGE

Include large product photography, alternate images, product name, price, description, fabric, colour, occasion, available stock, quantity selector, size or blouse information where relevant, Add to Cart, Buy Now and Order on WhatsApp buttons. Include related products and recently viewed products.

CART AND CHECKOUT

Build a functional cart drawer and cart page. Allow quantity updates, removal, subtotal calculation and order summary. Provide a checkout UI with customer name, phone number, address and delivery information. Include an option to continue ordering through WhatsApp. Do not pretend payments are active without a real payment gateway integration.

ADMIN DASHBOARD DEMO

Create a separate admin interface with product management, add/edit/delete product interactions, order list, order status, stock management and basic sales summary. Use demonstration data. Keep the customer storefront and admin dashboard visually separate.

WHATSAPP INTEGRATION

Add strategically placed WhatsApp ordering CTAs on product pages and throughout the shopping journey. Use a configurable placeholder phone number rather than inventing a real business number. Prepare prefilled product enquiry messages.

SEO AND PERFORMANCE

Implement semantic HTML, unique page titles, meta descriptions, clean URLs, product structured data where appropriate, Open Graph metadata, sitemap and robots.txt. Optimise images, lazy loading, responsive image sizes and page performance. Make the site mobile-first and accessible.

INTERACTIONS AND QUALITY

All buttons, navigation, filters, search, cart, wishlist, product quick views and menus must work in the prototype. Include smooth but restrained animations, elegant hover states and loading/empty states. Ensure accessibility and responsive layouts across desktop, tablet and mobile.

TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS

Use React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS and reusable components. Use a clean component structure and consistent design tokens. Use realistic saree product imagery from suitable high-quality image sources, not random unrelated stock photos. Do not use generated placeholder text, broken image links or fake functional payment integrations.

IMPORTANT CREATIVE DIRECTION

Do not create a generic Shopify-looking theme, a standard three-column AI-generated template, or a generic fashion landing page. Make deliberate design decisions for this specific saree business. Prioritise visual storytelling, premium merchandising, product photography, editorial composition and conversion-focused shopping. The result should be polished enough to present to a real business owner as a professional website concept.

First build the complete high-fidelity frontend prototype with realistic demo data and working interactions. Do not introduce unnecessary backend complexity until the design and user experience are approved.
